## Unreleased

**API**:

- API keys. Send one as `Authorization: Bearer <key>` and your limit rises from 60 requests a
  minute per IP address to 600 per key. Requests without a key keep working, unchanged and
  unauthenticated. A key that is unknown or revoked answers `401 unauthorized`, within a minute of
  revocation.
- Limits answer `429 rate_limited` with a `Retry-After` header, and every answer carries
  `RateLimit-Limit`, `RateLimit-Remaining` and `RateLimit-Reset`. See [API keys](/docs/guides/api-keys).
- A key identifies an integrator; it never authorises moving funds. The API stays non-custodial and
  never accepts a private key.

**SDK**: nothing changed. `createXebraClient({ apiKey })` now has an effect.

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-15)

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk` 0.4.0, `@xebra/sdk/intents`:

- `createRebalancer`: a market maker moves its own USDC inside NEAR Intents from one chain's asset
  ID to another's over CCTP (withdraw to its wallet, burn, 1Click deposit back to its account).
  `estimate` compares the cost with the same move as one swap inside NEAR Intents.
- `usdcChainOf` recognises Aptos USDC, and skips asset IDs 1Click marks `USDC(DEPRECATED)` (the
  HOT IDs that Omni IDs replaced on Avalanche, Polygon and BNB Chain on 2026-11-09).

The API did not change. Nothing in 0.3.0 changed.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-14)

**API**:

- Solana, Sui and Aptos are source chains to every EVM chain Xebra lists and to Arc; Sui and Aptos
  also send to Solana, and all three to Stellar, where Xebra's relayer completes the mint. `GET
  /v1/routes` lists them; `POST /v1/transfers` from Aptos returns a step of kind `aptos`.

- Transfers from Arc build again while Xebra's fee wrapper is on. `GET /v1/quote` from Arc now
  prices with the wrapper's live terms (its fee, minimum and maximum); before, it showed no Xebra fee
  and `POST /v1/transfers` refused. The approval and the burn go to the wrapper, and the burn is good
  for 30 minutes.
- EVM chains can send to each other, not only to and from Arc, wherever Xebra switches the pair on.
  `GET /v1/routes` lists them.

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk` 0.3.0:

- `EnabledRoutes.evmMesh` and `parseEvmMesh`: EVM chains that all send to each other.
- [NEAR Intents](/docs/guides/near-intents): `pickHub` and `buildDeposit` work from Arc and the EVM
  chains as they did from Stellar. The guide shows an EVM sender.
- `ChainId` includes `"aptos"`, and `TransferStep` a new `aptos` kind: Circle's precompiled CCTP V2
  burn script and its arguments. From `@xebra/sdk/build`, `aptosBurnPayload` turns it into what an
  Aptos wallet signs, and `aptosBurnArgs`, `buildAptosBurnStep` and `normaliseAptosAddress` build one.
- Sui: `buildForwardedBurn` and `buildSuiForwardStep` burn with Circle's forwarding, so Sui reaches
  every EVM chain and Solana without a claim on the other side.
- `stellarBurnRecipient("G...")`: the mint recipient and hook for a burn into Stellar from any chain.

Nothing in 0.2.0 changed.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-13)

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk` 0.2.0:

- `@xebra/sdk/intents`, for [NEAR Intents](/docs/guides/near-intents): `isRouteAvailable(from, to)`

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-12)

The first public release of the API and the SDK.

**API**, at `https://api.xebra.exchange/v1`:

- `GET /v1/routes`: every route Xebra sends on right now.
- `GET /v1/quote`: what a transfer costs and what the recipient gets.
- `POST /v1/transfers`: the unsigned transactions for a transfer, in signing order.
- `GET /v1/transfers/{chain}/{tx}`: where a transfer is, by its burn.
- `POST /v1/claims`: the unsigned mint for a delivery that did not complete on its own.
- `GET /v1/health`: liveness.

Errors are RFC 9457 problems with a stable `code`. The OpenAPI 3.1 document is published with the
SDK.

**SDK**, `@xebra/sdk` 0.1.0 on npm:

- `@xebra/sdk`: the typed API client, wire schemas and error codes.
- `@xebra/sdk/core`: chains, amounts, fee arithmetic, address checks and deep links.
- `@xebra/sdk/build`: build unsigned transactions against your own RPC.
- `@xebra/sdk/openapi`: the OpenAPI document.

## Planned

These are not released and have no dates yet:
